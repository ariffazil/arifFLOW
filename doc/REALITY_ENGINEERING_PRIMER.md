## Section 1: The Thesis

> **Governance becomes physics, not policy.**

- Governance is external — a checklist, a police force, a barrier
- Agents must "think about governance" — slowing them down
- Rules can be bypassed, forgotten, or misapplied
- The system degrades when governance is relaxed

In reality engineering:
- Governance is the medium — like water to a fish
- Agents move through governance without thinking about it
- Rules are enforced by the substrate, not by memory
- The system cannot degrade because governance IS the physics

Physical reality does not "check a policy" before letting a river flow downhill. The medium — gravity, viscosity, topology — shapes the flow. The river does not think about gravity. It just flows.

Your agents should move through governance the same way.
